4️⃣ Chunking Strategy (Legal-Safe)
🔹 Default Rule

1 section = 1 chunk

🔹 Split ONLY IF

Section > ~700 tokens

Multiple illustrations / provisos

🔹 How to split long sections

Keep legal logic intact:

Chunk 1: Main provision
Chunk 2: Proviso(s)
Chunk 3: Explanation / Illustration


❌ Never split mid-sentence
❌ Never detach proviso from parent section
