Phase 6 – Safety Layer (Legal Guardrails)

This phase controls model behavior, not intelligence.

1️⃣ Add System Prompt Constraints (Non-Negotiable)

This is the root authority over the model.

🔹 Mandatory System Prompt Rules

Your system prompt must enforce:

Answer only from retrieved legal context

Always cite Act + Section

Never give legal advice or opinions

No assumptions or hypotheticals

Refuse if information is missing

🔹 Example System Prompt (Core)
You are a legal information assistant for Indian laws.
You must answer strictly based on the provided legal text.
Always cite the relevant Act and Section.
Do not provide legal advice, opinions, or interpretations.
If the answer is not present in the context, say you do not know.

2️⃣ Block Advisory & Opinion-Based Language
🔹 Disallowed Phrases (Hard Block)

“You should…”

“I recommend…”

“In my opinion…”

“Best option is…”

“The accused is guilty…”

🔹 Allowed Neutral Language

“According to Section…”

“The Act states…”

“The punishment prescribed is…”

👉 Implement regex / keyword filters post-generation to catch leaks.

3️⃣ Add Legal Disclaimer (Mandatory for Deployment)
🔹 One-Time UI Disclaimer

This system provides legal information based on Indian statutes.
It does not provide legal advice or replace consultation with a qualified legal professional.

🔹 Inline Disclaimer (Optional for long answers)

Append for sensitive queries (criminal / punishment-related)

4️⃣ Handle Unknown & Unsafe Questions Gracefully
🔹 When to Refuse

Case-specific questions

Hypothetical judgments

Missing legal basis

Opinion-based queries

🔹 Safe Refusal Template
The requested information is not available in the provided legal text.
This system can only provide information based on statutory provisions.


❌ Never hallucinate
❌ Never guess sections

🧠 Output of Phase 6

✅ Hallucination-resistant model
✅ Legal-safe responses
✅ Deterministic refusals
✅ Courtroom-safe wording
