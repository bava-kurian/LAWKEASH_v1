📌 Phase 2 – Data Preparation (Detailed)
1️⃣ Collect Indian Bare Acts (Open-Source)

Sources (legal & free):

Government portals (e.g., Ministry of Law & Justice)

Open legal repositories (India Code, Legal PDFs)

Public GitHub datasets (law text archives)

Formats you’ll encounter:

PDF (scanned / text-based)

HTML

DOC / TXT

Best practice:

Prefer HTML/TXT over scanned PDFs

Maintain a master metadata file:

{
  "act_name": "Indian Penal Code",
  "year": 1860,
  "source": "India Code",
  "format": "HTML"
}

2️⃣ Clean and Normalize Legal Text

Legal text is noisy. Cleaning is critical for embeddings & retrieval.

🔹 Remove Noise

Headers & footers

Page numbers

“As amended by Act XX of YYYY”

Excess whitespace & line breaks

🔹 Normalize Text

Convert to UTF-8

Lowercase (optional – depends on tokenizer)

Standardize symbols:

Section— → Section

: ; – → consistent punctuation

🔹 Preserve Legal Structure

❌ Don’t remove:

Section numbers

Sub-clauses (a), (b), (i)

Definitions (“means”, “includes”)

✅ Example cleaned text:

Section 420 Cheating and dishonestly inducing delivery of property.
Whoever cheats and thereby dishonestly induces the person deceived to deliver any property...

3️⃣ Split Laws into Semantic Chunks

This is not line-based splitting — it’s meaning-based.

🔹 Chunking Strategy (Recommended)

Primary unit: Section

Secondary split: Long sections → paragraphs / clauses

🔹 Ideal Chunk Size

300–700 tokens per chunk

Avoid breaking:

Definitions mid-sentence

Exceptions (Provided that...)

Illustrations

🔹 Example Chunk
Act: Indian Penal Code, 1860
Section: 420
Chunk:
Whoever cheats and thereby dishonestly induces the person deceived to deliver any property to any person...

4️⃣ Label Chunks with Act + Section (Very Important)

Every chunk must be traceable back to law.

🔹 Metadata Schema (Must-Have)
{
  "act": "Indian Penal Code",
  "year": 1860,
  "section": "420",
  "title": "Cheating and dishonestly inducing delivery of property",
  "chunk_id": "IPC_420_01",
  "text": "Whoever cheats and thereby dishonestly induces..."
}

🔹 Why This Matters

Enables legal citations

Helps explainability (“According to Section 420 IPC…”)

Improves RAG accuracy

Required for courtroom / compliance use cases

5️⃣ Validation & Quality Check

Before moving to embeddings:

✅ Checklist:

No missing section numbers

No mixed acts inside one chunk

Chunk size within limits

Metadata completeness

Legal meaning preserved

Optional:

Run NER to detect:

Acts

Sections

Legal terms
