📌 Phase 5 – Model Training
1️⃣ Select Base LLM (Foundation Model)

Your base model must already be strong in language + reasoning, so fine-tuning only teaches Indian legal behavior, not grammar.

🔹 Recommended Base Models

LLaMA 2 (7B / 13B)

Mistral 7B

Mixtral 8x7B (if resources allow)

Phi-2 (lightweight experiments)

🔹 Selection Criteria
Criterion	Why it matters
Open weights	Required for LoRA
Strong instruction following	Legal answers need structure
Context length	Law sections can be long
Hardware fit	7B works on single GPU

👉 Best balance: Mistral 7B or LLaMA 2 7B

2️⃣ Apply LoRA Fine-Tuning (Efficient & Safe)

Instead of full fine-tuning, you inject legal knowledge via adapters.

🔹 Why LoRA is Mandatory

💾 100× fewer parameters

🧠 Prevents catastrophic forgetting

⚖️ Easier rollback if outputs degrade

🔐 Safer for legal domain

🔹 What LoRA Learns

✅ Legal phrasing
✅ Section referencing behavior
✅ Answer structure
❌ Does NOT rewrite base knowledge

🔹 LoRA Configuration (Recommended)
lora_r: 16
lora_alpha: 32
lora_dropout: 0.05
target_modules:
  - q_proj
  - v_proj


Frameworks:

Hugging Face Transformers

PEFT

3️⃣ Train on Indian Legal Instruction Dataset

This uses your Phase 3 dataset.

🔹 Training Data Format (Strict)
{
  "instruction": "What is the punishment for cheating under IPC?",
  "input": "",
  "response": "As per Section 420 of the Indian Penal Code, cheating and dishonestly inducing delivery of property is punishable with imprisonment of either description for a term which may extend to seven years, and shall also be liable to fine."
}

🔹 Training Rules (Non-Negotiable)

✅ Always mention:

Act name

Section number

❌ Never generate:

Opinions

Case outcomes

Judicial interpretations

🔹 Hyperparameters (Safe Defaults)
Parameter	Value
Epochs	2–3
Batch size	2–4
LR	2e-4
Optimizer	AdamW
Precision	fp16 / bf16

⚠️ Over-training = hallucination risk

4️⃣ Add Legal Guardrails During Training
🔹 Response Pattern Reinforcement

Teach the model to always respond like this:

According to Section ___ of the ___ Act, ...

🔹 Refusal Training (Optional but Powerful)

Add examples like:

Instruction:

Is Section 498A misused?

Response:

This question is opinion-based. The Indian Penal Code only defines the legal provisions under Section 498A and does not assess misuse.

This makes your model legally conservative.

5️⃣ Evaluate Output Clarity & Correctness

This phase is more important than loss values.

🔹 Automatic Evaluation

Check:

Section hallucination ❌

Wrong punishment ❌

Missing act name ❌

Over-verbosity ❌

Metrics:

Exact section match

Answer grounding score

Token overlap with source

🔹 Manual Legal Evaluation (Mandatory)

Sample ~50 outputs and verify:

Check	Pass?
Correct section	✅
Correct punishment	✅
Neutral tone	✅
No assumptions	✅
Clear language	✅
🔹 Good vs Bad Output

❌ Bad

Cheating usually leads to jail depending on circumstances.

✅ Good

According to Section 420 of the Indian Penal Code, cheating and dishonestly inducing delivery of property is punishable with imprisonment of either description for a term which may extend to seven years, and shall also be liable to fine.
